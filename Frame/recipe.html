Cheesy Pizza

Crispy, cheesy and delicious homemade pizza recipe.


Recipe Information

Preparation Time 20 Minutes
Cooking Time 25 Minutes
Servings 4 Persons
Difficulty Easy
Calories 320 kcal

Ingredients

  • 2 Pizza Bases
  • 1 Cup Mozzarella Cheese
  • 1 Onion (Chopped)
  • 1 Capsicum (Chopped)
  • 1 Tomato (Chopped)
  • 1 Cup Sweet Corn
  • Pizza Sauce
  • Mixed Herbs
  • Chilli Flakes
  • Olive Oil

Preparation Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 200°C.
  2. Spread pizza sauce on the pizza base.
  3. Add chopped vegetables evenly.
  4. Sprinkle mozzarella cheese generously.
  5. Add herbs and chilli flakes.
  6. Bake for 20–25 minutes.
  7. Slice and serve hot.

Nutrition Facts

Nutrient Amount
Calories 320 kcal
Protein 14 g
Carbohydrates 38 g
Fat 12 g
Calcium 250 mg

Chef's Tips

  • Use fresh mozzarella cheese for better taste.
  • Do not overload the pizza with toppings.
  • Brush olive oil on the crust before baking.
  • Serve immediately for the best flavor.
